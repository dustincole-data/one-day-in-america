# 08 · The reference wall and the honest positioning

Assembled 2026-08-10 · claude (opus) · browsable page: [`wall.html`](wall.html) (served locally, see Method)

**What exists now:** 22 real references captured from live pages, 4 generated mood frames, 3 anti-references,
and a positioning statement that names which portfolio mechanic this piece plays and why it is not a rerun.
All of it is *gravity, not direction* — Fable may reject every image on the wall.

---

## 1 · The positioning (the ruling)

**This site is:** one page, one mark. 7,669 real 2024 diary days, one thread each, 4 a.m. → 4 a.m., coloured by
activity. Solid at 3 a.m., frayed through the middle of the day. That contrast is the entire argument and it
has to land before the viewer touches anything. Filter-the-rope and pull-one-thread is the second act.

**This site is not:** an atlas (nothing to browse) · about you (no input, no personalisation) · a scroll story
(no chapters, no narrated reveal) · a year comparison (2024 only) · live (no clock, no "right now") · a chart
whose legend does the work.

**The mechanic it plays: the beautiful object.** The four mechanics already in the portfolio:

| Mechanic | Shipped as | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Scale gasp | Deep Time — 115,000 px at true scale | a magnitude you cannot argue with |
| It's-about-you | Namesake (type a name), Climate Fingerprint (type a city) | the data becomes yours |
| You-produce-the-insight | Statistical Illusions, By Example, The Real Price of Everything | you cause the finding |
| **Beautiful object** | Climate Fingerprint's posters, Why Do They's blooms, Where America Moves' chord | worth looking at before it is read |

**The difference that keeps it from being a rerun: the object's coming-apart *is* the finding.** No shipped
project has a mark whose structural failure carries the claim. It also borrows the *timing* of the scale gasp
— one instant, no interaction — while the gasp itself is about pattern, not size.

| Not a rerun of | Because |
|---|---|
| Climate Fingerprint | an atlas of 30+ posters, one per city, parameterised by the viewer; comparison happens *across* objects. Here: one object, comparison *inside* it (3 a.m. and noon in the same frame) |
| Where America Moves | explore-first — the chord means nothing until a metro is clicked. Here the untouched view is already the claim |
| Why Do They | its punch is words you read. This one is wordless; the punch is texture |
| Deep Time | its gasp is magnitude. Here both ends of the frame hold the *same* 7,669 threads — nothing grows, it comes apart |
| Namesake / By Example | both require input before anything happens. This requires none |

## 3 · Mood frames (gpt-image-1, 4 images, `gen/`)

Generated only for the thing no existing reference shows: a rope of individual day-threads, tight at one hour
and frayed at another. **Texture and feeling only — no data, not mockups.** Each caption on the wall states what
the frame gets wrong so it cannot be read as a spec:

- **m1-dark-braid** — the braid on black. *Wrong:* colours run as a continuous left-to-right spectrum (a ramp,
  not 12 separable majors), and the fray is ordered by horizontal position rather than by activity.
- **m2-light-braid** — the braid on warm paper, pigment rather than light. *Wrong:* the "solid" band became a
  filled slab; in the real mark, agreement is still 7,669 separate threads that happen to align.
- **m3-phone-braid** — portrait frame, tight at both ends, open through the middle (mobile parity is a hard
  gate). *Wrong:* the threads bow outward like a spindle — a shape imposed on the data, not produced by it.
- **m4-fray-macro** — overlapping translucent strands at pixel level, twelve distinguishable hues. Shows the
  payoff and the risk together: additive blending makes density visible as brightness and then compounds to
  white; on a light ground the same move goes to mud.

Prompts are recorded verbatim at the bottom of this file so the frames can be regenerated.

## 4 · Anti-references (3)

1. **FlowingData, *A Day in the Life of Americans* (2015)** — the dot simulation. Same dataset and the most-shared
   piece in this space, so a remake reads as a remake. Structurally it also fails this spine: it makes the viewer
   *wait* for the day to play, while the claim must land in five seconds and hold still; and each dot is a
   simulation token, not a real diary day that can be pulled out.
2. **The activity-transition hairball** (same post) — tangle without structure reads as noise. It fails the
   wordless test on sight: you cannot tell the ordered end from the disordered end, which is the one thing the
   viewer has to see.
3. **NYT, *How Different Groups Spend Their Day* (2009)** — the canonical ATUS stacked area. Aggregating every
   person into smooth bands destroys the fray (the claim) and the single thread (the exploration layer) in one
   move. Honest bonus: the interactive is a dead Flash object today — captured as such. That is the expected
   chart's afterlife.
4. *(no image)* **A decorative neon particle field** — glow and swirl with no data mapping. The AI-artifact tell;
   here every strand on screen has to be one real diary day.

## 5 · What the wall deliberately does not do

- **Prescribes no mark, palette, ground, layout, type or technology**, and takes no position on 2D vs 3D.
- **Impeccable and Intent were not used** to assemble it and must not shape the design (thin anchor holds).
  A late mobile / a11y / perf QA pass is the only role available to them.
- **Carries no render budget and no data shape** — those travel in the ticket 09 brief. Only three constraints
  from the claim record touch what the wall may suggest, and they are quoted verbatim on the page: **C3** (fine
  activity partition; a coarse palette makes the headline false), **C10** (the mandatory source line, which is
  the no-simultaneity defence), **C11** ("Nobody's" is safe over the mark and unsafe without it).

## 6 · Decisions — W1–W6, binding ticket 09

| # | Decision | Why | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|---|
| **W1** | **The mechanic is the beautiful object, and the object's coming-apart is the finding.** The brief says this in one line and does not shop the other three mechanics | Every other mechanic is already spoken for by a shipped project, and three of them require input or a sequence this spine cannot afford | Pitching a quiz, a scroll story, a personalised view, or a "type your own X" hook |
| **W2** | **The wall ships as references + positioning only.** No prescribed mark, palette, ground, layout, type, or dimensionality | Thin anchor, locked at charting; anchor-before-mocks is a paid-for lesson but the anchor is references, not a form | Any sentence in the ticket-09 brief that tells Fable what the mark should be |
| **W3** | **The mood frames are labelled synthetic and each carries its own defect line** | A generated frame with no defect line becomes a spec by accident; m1's spectrum ramp would silently violate C3 | Sending the frames to Fable without their captions |
| **W4** | **The three anti-references are justified by locked decisions, not taste** — remake risk, the wordless test, and the aggregation that destroys both the fray and the thread | An anti-reference on taste alone is house style entering by the back door | Adding anti-references that only say "I don't like it" |
| **W5** | **The 2009 NYT ATUS interactive is dead (Flash) and is captured as dead** | It is the canonical prior art and cannot be inspected live; saying so is more useful than a reconstruction | Reconstructing it to have a prettier anti-reference |
| **W6** | **The wall is a local artifact, not a site page** — it lives in `research/08-reference-wall/` and is served locally | It is design input, not content; the deployed site stays `noindex` until the piece ships | Publishing the wall to the Vercel project or the subdomain |

## Method / provenance

- Captured 2026-08-10 with headless Chrome (puppeteer-core, own profile — the shared MCP profile is contended).
  Full-page captures for case-study pages, viewport captures for interactives; JPEG, resized to 1000 px wide.
- Discovery was done by loading the hub pages (`visualcinnamon.com/portfolio`, `truth-and-beauty.net`,
  `pudding.cool`, `datasketch.es`, `awwwards.com`) and reading their real link lists — no URLs typed from memory,
  no search-result domains opened.
- Every capture was eyeballed as a contact sheet before it went on the wall; four candidates were dropped for
  showing nothing useful (Dear Data, giorgialupi.com, Pudding *Happy Map*, VC *Breathing Earth*).
- Images are screenshots of the linked pages, held for design reference only. Mood frames are synthetic.

### Mood-frame prompts (verbatim)

**m1-dark-braid** (1536×1024, high): *Abstract data art on a deep near-black ground. Roughly seven thousand
hair-thin threads run from the bottom edge of the frame to the top edge, packed shoulder to shoulder into one
dense vertical rope that fills the width of the frame. Across the lower third every thread is exactly parallel
and shares one deep indigo tone, so the rope reads as a single solid luminous band with a faint glow. Through
the middle of the frame the threads separate into twelve clearly distinct saturated hues, amber and coral and
teal and magenta and lime and violet and rust and sky blue and gold and jade and rose and cobalt, each thread
changing colour at its own height, so the solid band dissolves into a fine coloured fray in which individual
strands stay traceable. Translucent strokes, light adding where strands cross, subtle vertical grain, a purely
graphic unlabelled surface, gallery print quality, extremely fine linework.*

**m2-light-braid** (1536×1024, high): same structure on *a warm paper-white ground … one soft slate-blue pigment
… twelve clearly distinct pigment hues, ochre and vermilion and viridian and plum and olive and cobalt and
terracotta and pale gold and rose and sage and ultramarine and mustard … translucent overlapping ink, the paper
visible between strands … riso print texture, extremely fine linework.*

**m3-phone-braid** (1024×1536, high): *…a tall narrow portrait frame … the composition built for a phone screen.
Thousands of hair-thin threads run the full height … The bottom quarter and the top quarter are perfectly
parallel and share one deep indigo tone … The middle half of the frame opens into a fine fray of twelve distinct
saturated hues … individual strands traceable inside it.*

**m4-fray-macro** (1536×1024, high): *Extreme macro close-up of a few hundred hair-thin translucent coloured
threads crossing one another, filling the whole frame, on a deep near-black ground … Where two threads overlap
the colour brightens toward white, where many overlap the light blooms, so density itself is visible as
luminosity. Some threads run straight and parallel, others wander and tangle, and the eye can follow one single
strand across the frame.*
