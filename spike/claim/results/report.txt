C3 / C4 — how much of the braid is COORDINATION and how much is arithmetic?
====================================================================================
The hostile reading: "of course the country agrees at 3 a.m. — everyone sleeps 8 hours, and of
course workers work at noon — you filtered on working." Both are answerable with the same null:
hold every person's activity durations fixed and rotate their day to a uniform random start.
Coordination is then observed ÷ expected-under-rotation, and the expectation is analytic —
E[share doing X at any minute] = weighted mean minutes of X ÷ 1440.

  everyone asleep, 3:00 a.m.               observed  95.7%   rotation-null  37.7%    2.54×
  everyone on personal care, 3:00 a.m.     observed  96.4%   rotation-null  40.8%    2.36×
  everyone working, 12:00 p.m.             observed  24.4%   rotation-null  14.3%    1.71×
  worked 6h+ weekday, working 12:00 p.m.   observed  65.3%   rotation-null  39.2%    1.67×
  worked 6h+ weekday, working 2:00 p.m.    observed  86.4%   rotation-null  39.2%    2.20×

So the night is real coordination, not sleep arithmetic — and the workers' midday tightening is
not the filter talking either: the filter guarantees one work minute, not a shared window.
