# 07 · Freeze the spine's claim

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 06

## Question

Is the number this site asserts defensible in public, and what exactly may we say about it?

Run `rigor-claim` on the locked spine. Produce the frozen claim record:

- The **exact number**, its population, its year(s), its weighting, and its uncertainty.
- The **causal-language register** — what verb is honest. ATUS is cross-sectional; "the commute
  time went to work, not leisure" is a different claim from "people chose to work more." Get the
  register right before any word ships.
- **What the claim cannot support** — the obvious misreading a viewer will make, and whether the
  design has to actively prevent it.
- The **adversarial language pass** on whatever few words appear on screen.

This gates the Fable brief: Fable must not be sent a spine that later turns out unsayable.
