Type: task (HITL)
Status: open
Blocked by: 09

## Question

Which direction gets built, and what does the finished design have to specify before code starts?

- Put Fable's 3–4 directions to Dustin. He picks **one**. Grafting the best idea from a runner-up
  is allowed; building two is not.
- Send Fable back into the winner for depth. What must come back before the build can begin:
  **desktop and phone both** (phone is not a squeezed desktop), the palette, the type, the motion
  and its trigger, the interaction grammar including touch, all states (loading, empty, no-JS,
  reduced-motion), and what the first five seconds do before the viewer touches anything.
- Sanity-check the deep design against the ticket-04 budget before accepting it. A design that
  can't hit the frame budget on a phone is not accepted, however beautiful.

Resolving this ticket clears the fog on the build: the implementation tickets get charted here.
